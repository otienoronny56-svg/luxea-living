Frequently Asked Questions

What currency are prices shown in? +

All prices default to Kenyan Shillings (KSh). You can switch to USD, EUR, or GBP anytime using the header selector.

How do I book a stay or hotel room? +

Pick your dates on Stays or Hotels and click Reserve. Your dates are held immediately and you can confirm via WhatsApp or M-Pesa.

How do hosts and hotels list on Luxea? +

Go to the Host page, submit your property or hotel details, and our team reviews within 24 hours. Once approved, you get a calendar, client ledger, and laundry dispatch.

How does the Laundry Valet work? +

Guests and hosts can book doorstep wash, dry cleaning, or linen turnover in KSh on the Laundry page.

How do reviews and Trust Credits work? +

Guests can rate stays and laundry partners or report an issue. Good reviews earn Trust Credits that boost top hosts on Luxea.
